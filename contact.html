HOW GLOWHAUS BEGAN

A softer kind
of skincare.

GlowHaus began with a simple thought: taking care of your skin shouldn’t come with pressure to change it. In this fictional studio story, Cape Town esthetician Nora Ellis opened the first GlowHaus room in 2019 after hearing clients ask for less judgment and more kindness.

She started with one treatment bed, a short menu of thoughtful facials, and a promise to listen before recommending anything. As word spread, the little room grew into a neighborhood studio shaped around the same idea: good care should feel personal, unrushed, and comfortable for the person receiving it.

Today, that spirit still guides every visit. We’ll meet your skin where it is, share what we know, and leave the pressure at the door.
